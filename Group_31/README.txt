GROUP 31 TERM PAPER FILES

Research_Report.pdf and Research_Report.docx: full paper with four figures.
report/IEEE_Report.pdf and report/IEEE_Report.docx: concise IEEE-style paper.
slides/Presentation.pptx: 11-slide presentation with editable charts.
figures/: four standalone figures.
analysis.ipynb and analysis_group31.py: reproducible calculations.
Group_31_weighted_results.csv and Group_31_exploratory_model.csv: output tables.
data/: India Global Findex 2025 version IND_2024_FINDEX_v02_M.

To reproduce: open analysis.ipynb with this Group_31 folder as the working directory, or run python analysis_group31.py from this folder. Python requires pandas and numpy. The analysis reads the data/ file and rewrites the two CSV outputs.

The World Bank's microdata terms restrict redistribution of the survey file and require research use, respondent confidentiality, and source citation. Consult https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/7916/get-microdata before sharing the data. The work uses the supplied survey weights; the logistic model's uncertainty assumes independent observations and is not a full survey-design calculation.

The DOCX layout was not rendered because LibreOffice was unavailable on this computer. The PDFs were independently typeset and are the checked versions; review DOCX formatting in Word before submission.
