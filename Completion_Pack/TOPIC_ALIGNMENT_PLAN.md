# Plan: align the project with the actual topic

**Actual topic (confirmed by the group, 20 Nov 2026):** Mobile banking: financial inclusion vs cybersecurity risks.

The paper's earlier title ("Mobile Banking, Financial Inclusion and Cybersecurity Risk in India: ... Institutional Payment-Safety Service Proposal") put the venture proposal in the title and did not frame inclusion and cyber risk as a tension. Decisions:

1. **Title:** "Mobile Banking: Financial Inclusion vs Cybersecurity Risks" with the subtitle "Evidence from India's 2024 Global Findex, public app reviews and published interview studies, with implications for entrepreneurs". Applied to the paper, slides, IEEE report, notebook, README, CONTEXT and file metadata.
2. **Framing:** the question is whether more inclusion (account ownership, digital payment use) goes with more cyber risk (reported scam exposure, weak device protection, fraud grievances), and for whom. The venture proposal stays, as the managerial implication (Section 5.3), not as the headline.
3. **Quantitative (new):** an inclusion-vs-risk analysis by group (payment use against reported exposure and lock use), with a figure and table; the existing weighted tables, logistic model, peer comparison and design-effect sensitivity stay.
4. **Web data (new):** the app reviews so far are mostly UPI payment apps. "Mobile banking" means bank apps, so reviews of major bank mobile-banking apps (Google Play and Apple App Store) are scraped and analysed, and compared with the payment apps.
5. **Qualitative:** same theme codebook on the bank-app reviews, a manual classification of fraud-keyword reviews with an independent blind second pass, plus the five published studies already synthesised.
6. **Rebuild** the paper, slides and IEEE report; re-run the consistency checks; update CONTEXT, README and notebook; commit.
